Repository name: fotrix-hydrogen-monitor

Description:
Flutter operator dashboard for FOTRIX, a solar-powered electrocoagulation-photocatalysis reactor for hydrogen production and microplastic degradation (PKM student project, Universitas Indonesia) — Firestore-backed device control, a simulated sensor readout, and a mission/safety screen.

Suggested topics:
flutter, firebase, firestore, iot, sensor-monitoring, dart, android, pkm
